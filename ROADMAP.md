## Where things stand

1. **Active paper (RESEARCH_PLAN).**  The finite algebra, the exact
   order-three shear-fiber classification, the active common metric-three-jet
   family, the fourth-order physical recovery, and the finite coordinate
   detector are compiled in Lean.  The single external dependency is the
   analytic EMD involutivity proposition (specialist audit = Gate P1); the
   positive benchmark gate P2 is closed; submission hardening is P3.
2. **Arc program (KALUZA_ARC_PLAN).**  Zero-claim groundwork is underway:
   normal-point contracted Bianchi and Noether identities, phase
   propagation, half-angle charts, and conditional fixed-choice recognition
   endpoints are compiled; representative construction, normal-chart
   existence, covariance, and all K-ledger rows remain open.
3. **Validation.**  Pinned exact-arithmetic benchmarks V1--V5, including the
   EMD symbol/Cartan certificate supporting the audit package.

## Stop rules

- No claim may exceed the evidence class recorded in the claim ledger.
- The active paper's plan governs work until P1--P3 close; the arc plan's
  Section 11 lists the only zero-claim exemptions.
- Degenerate-branch and global work follow, never precede, the gates above.
